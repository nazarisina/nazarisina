```text
Sina Nazari

├── Building: Cloud products, product teams, AI agents & useful automations
├── Copilots: ChatGPT Codex + Claude Code
└── Motto:    Stay curious. Ship thoughtfully. Automate the boring parts.
```

I started close to the metal, moved up through application code, and eventually found my favorite abstraction layer: **the product**. Today I lead product at [ParsPack](https://parspack.com/), helping teams turn difficult cloud-infrastructure problems into products people can actually understand and use.

I may spend more time with roadmaps than registers now—but I still love making things.
